PonderSource apps privacy notice

Effective 17 November 2026

Stichting Ponder Source operates the PonderSource app launcher at pondersource.com and the Litewrite, Inbox RS, Hyperdraft, Sharesome, Webmarks, Todonna, Inspektor, Savr and Marco apps on its subdomains. For privacy questions or requests, contact michiel@unhosted.org.

Your app data

These apps run in your browser. They store app data on your device and, when you choose to connect storage, synchronize data with your selected remoteStorage provider, Google Drive or Dropbox. This can include notes, bookmarks, saved articles, tasks, uploaded files, place bookmarks, app settings and your launcher library. Connecting storage may synchronize data already saved in the app.

The storage connection uses your account identifier, file contents and metadata, and authorization tokens to provide the app's storage and synchronization features. Tokens and cached data can be retained in browser storage so that the connection continues between visits. Your storage account password is entered with your provider, rather than into these apps.

Google Drive

The current remoteStorage.js integration requests permission to see, edit, create and delete all your Google Drive files. That permission is broader than a single app folder. The apps use Drive for their storage and synchronization features; Inspektor lets you inspect and manage data in the connected storage. Google Drive does not support the public-sharing features of this integration.

PonderSource uses Google user data to provide the visible features you choose to use. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. We do not allow people to read it except with your explicit permission for specific data, when necessary for security, or when required by law. Data is transferred only to provide features you choose, for necessary security purposes, or as required by law. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox and remoteStorage

Our Dropbox connection is limited to the PonderSource Apps folder in your Dropbox account. The apps share this folder so that compatible apps can access the same data. With remoteStorage, each app requests access to its relevant data categories; Inspektor requests broad access so that you can inspect your storage.

Sharing and other services

Storage synchronization sends app data directly between your browser and the storage provider. PonderSource does not operate a central database for this synchronized content. If you choose a public-sharing or publishing feature, the selected content may become accessible to anyone with its public link. Other PonderSource apps that you connect to the same provider may access compatible data.

Features such as maps, place search, article retrieval, link previews and externally hosted media contact the relevant third-party services. Those services receive the requests needed for the feature, which may include search terms, coordinates or URLs, and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Hosting and storage providers also process network requests under their own policies. This notice does not replace those providers' privacy policies.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it through the app or clear that site's browser data. Synchronized data remains with your provider until deleted there or through the app. Deletions may synchronize to other devices. Your provider's trash, version history and backup rules may retain copies.

You can disconnect an app and revoke its access in your provider's account settings. Disconnecting or revoking access does not itself delete stored files. Clearing browser storage can remove unsynchronized changes. Removing an app shortcut from the launcher does not delete that app's data. To request help with data held directly by PonderSource, contact the address above; you can manage provider-held files in your storage account.

Security and changes

Hosted apps and provider connections use HTTPS. Browser storage and provider permissions protect access, but these integrations do not add end-to-end encryption to your files. Protect your device and storage account accordingly.

We will update this notice when these practices change. If a change introduces a new use of Google user data, we will explain it and obtain consent before using the data in that new way.

Media Kraken

Track watched movies and your watch-later collection on your Solid Pod.

Open Media KrakenGo to your app launcher

Track movies and what to watch next

Keep a record of movies you have watched and a collection of films to watch later. Media Kraken saves your collection to your connected storage.

  • Track watched movies
  • Keep a watch-later collection
  • Save your collection to your chosen storage

Get started

Open Media Kraken to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Media Kraken.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

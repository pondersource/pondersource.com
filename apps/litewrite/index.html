Litewrite

Unobtrusive writing. A simple, distraction-free notepad that works offline and everywhere.

Open LitewriteGo to your app launcher

Distraction-free notes

Write notes without a busy interface. Litewrite is a lightweight notepad for capturing ideas and working offline.

  • Write and edit text notes
  • Keep writing offline
  • Use remoteStorage to keep your notes in your own storage

Get started

Open Litewrite to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Litewrite.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

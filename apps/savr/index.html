Save articles for offline reading

Build a reading list for later. Savr keeps saved articles on your device and can sync them with remoteStorage.

  • Save articles to read later
  • Read saved articles offline
  • Keep articles locally or sync with remoteStorage

Get started

Open Savr to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Savr.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

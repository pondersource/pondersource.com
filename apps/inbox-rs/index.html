Inbox RS

A universal inbox for saving URLs, notes, images, emails, and audio, backed by remoteStorage so your data stays yours.

Open Inbox RSGo to your app launcher

A universal inbox

Collect things you want to keep in one inbox: links to revisit, quick notes, images, emails and audio.

  • Save URLs and notes
  • Collect images, emails and audio
  • Keep your inbox in remoteStorage

Get started

Open Inbox RS to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Inbox RS.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

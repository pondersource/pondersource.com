Hyperdraft

Turn your text notes into a website. Organise your thinking and network your ideas with a simple double-bracket syntax.

Open HyperdraftGo to your app launcher

Notes that become a website

Build a connected collection of text notes. Hyperdraft uses double-bracket links to connect ideas and lets you turn notes into a website.

  • Write and organise text notes
  • Connect ideas with double-bracket links
  • Publish notes as a website

Get started

Open Hyperdraft to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Hyperdraft.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

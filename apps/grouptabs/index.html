Grouptabs

Track and split shared expenses, then share a tab with your group.

Open GrouptabsGo to your app launcher

Split group expenses

Share a tab for a trip, household or group activity. Open Grouptabs and choose Start using Grouptabs. The app syncs shared tabs through its CouchDB service.

  • Record who paid and who participated
  • Share a tab with friends
  • Keep working offline after opening a tab

Get started

Open Grouptabs to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Grouptabs.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

Sharesome

Share files quickly from your own remoteStorage account.

Open SharesomeGo to your app launcher

File sharing from your own storage

Share a file from your remoteStorage account. Sharesome provides a focused interface for making files available to others.

  • Share files from remoteStorage
  • Keep shared files in your own account

Get started

Open Sharesome to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Sharesome.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

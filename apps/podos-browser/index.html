Browse linked data

Open a resource URL to explore the linked data web. Sign in separately in PodOS Browser to work with resources on a Solid Pod.

  • Explore public RDF resources
  • Browse your Solid Pod
  • Create and edit linked data

Get started

Open PodOS Browser to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install PodOS Browser.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

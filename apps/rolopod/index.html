RoloPod

Keep a private address book in your Solid Pod and share selected contacts.

Open RoloPodGo to your app launcher

A private address book

Keep contacts in an address book on your Solid Pod. RoloPod manages its own Solid sign-in and supports sharing selected parts of your address book.

  • Collect and organise contacts
  • Store your address book in a Solid Pod
  • Share selected contacts

Get started

Open RoloPod to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install RoloPod.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

Umai

Collect recipes, plan your cooking, and save your cookbook to your Solid Pod.

Your recipe collection

Bring recipes together in a personal cookbook. Umai helps you collect recipes and plan your cooking, with your cookbook saved to a Solid Pod.

  • Collect recipes
  • Plan your cooking
  • Save your cookbook to your Solid Pod

Get started

Open Umai to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Umai.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

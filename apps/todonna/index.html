Simple to-do and shopping lists

Keep track of everyday tasks or your next shopping trip with a small, focused list app.

  • Create to-do lists
  • Keep a shopping list
  • Use a simple interface without ads

Get started

Open Todonna to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Todonna.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

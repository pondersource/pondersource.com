Tasks, lists and workspaces

Create tasks, set priorities and due dates, and organize your work into lists and workspaces. Try Focus as a guest or connect storage to keep your projects across devices.

  • Tasks with priorities and due dates
  • Lists and workspaces for different projects
  • Complete guest project export and import

Get started

Open Focus to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install Focus.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.

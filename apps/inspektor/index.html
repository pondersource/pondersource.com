RS Inspektor

Browse your remoteStorage data

See what is inside your remoteStorage account. RS Inspektor is a tool for browsing folders, previewing files and working with JSON data.

  • Browse storage folders
  • Preview files
  • Inspect and edit JSON

Get started

Open RS Inspektor to use the app on its own website. To keep a shortcut, visit the Ponder Source launcher, choose App store, and install RS Inspektor.

Installing adds a shortcut to your launcher. Removing it does not delete your data in the app.
